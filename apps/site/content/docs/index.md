# What is Terraverse?

Terraverse is an open-source voxel universe. Its planets are generated from plates, erosion, climate and rivers; its matter melts, burns and boils because of what it is; and its machines are built from parts and joints and driven by real physics.

On top of that universe you play games: survival, racing, or whatever the community makes. Every world starts from a **world code**, so anyone can stand on the same planet.
