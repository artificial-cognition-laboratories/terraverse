**Terraverse 0.1.17: your account, friends and joining**

- A new Account page in the Library: edit your bio, location, website and links from the game.
- Handles are chosen once, when you first sign in on the site.
- The account icon sits beside the Library search; its popover lists friends, invites and remembered accounts, all searchable.
- Invites now show in the popover with Join and ✕. Joining a friend works from inside a world too: it saves, leaves and joins.
- Play as: start a second game as another remembered account.
- Fewer flashes of an old frame in the menu.
