**Terraverse 0.1.18: the land to a sixteenth**

- The ground is finer: land is shaped to a sixteenth of a block, so slopes and hills are smooth rather than stairs.
- Natural soil and rock follow one smooth surface across the land.
- Agents in your world: worlds you open are listed for `terra attach`, and the palette's `*` brings Axon's agents in.
- Uses less memory: about a third less RAM, and crashes now write a report beside the log.
- The far view keeps detail by on-screen size, from close up to the horizon; near terrain draws leaner.
- Fixed a crash on shutdown, and drawing on older OpenGL drivers.
