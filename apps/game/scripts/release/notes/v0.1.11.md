**Terra Firma 0.1.11: coasts with gulfs, landforms, and a smoother frame**

- Coasts are cut by the sea: it floods low ground into gulfs and inland seas, and waves wear soft rock into bays. TF24 world codes will grow different land again.
- New landforms where the ground and climate suit them: inselbergs, mesas and buttes, cuestas, tower karst, badlands, cinder cones and drumlins.
- No more invisible wall at the planet's seams between grids, and no holes in the ground where its mesh detail changes.
- Your figure is lit properly under the open sky, instead of turning black.
- The chat log no longer sits over the game; chat comes back as part of the UI.
- Faster frames in the far view and planetarium, and the planetarium frames and tints the planet better on hover.
