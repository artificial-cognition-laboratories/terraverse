**Terra Firma 0.1.6: back to the skeleton, and mods in TypeScript**

- The game is cut back to its foundations while it's rebuilt on TypeScript mods: crafting, hand tools, kindling, charcoal, fired clay and the Lab are gone for now. The planet, its matter, weather, sky and machines all still run.
- The first official TypeScript mod: flint, timber, aluminium, steel, rubber, canvas and glass as building blocks.
- Chat commands: /msg, /players, /kick and /announce.
- Creative gives you flying, fast movement, noclip and instant digging. Sprinting and the map are built into the game.
- The land is mid-rework: a world code made in 0.1.4 (TF24) may now grow different land.
