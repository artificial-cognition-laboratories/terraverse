**Terra Firma 0.1.13: digging by shape, and a new interface**

- Digging works in shapes: bore a round hole, or clear a box, a pit or everything above a level, cut at sixteenths and snapped to the grid, to your feet or to the ground you're aiming at.
- The HUD, hotbar, inventory and chat have been redone.
- `terra serve` runs a world as a server that you can join from this machine.
