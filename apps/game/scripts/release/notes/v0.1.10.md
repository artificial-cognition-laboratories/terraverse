**Terra Firma 0.1.10: smoother menus, and land with a past**

- The menus and HUD are drawn straight from the GPU, in step with the game's frames, so they no longer stutter or lag behind.
- The start of land with a history: plates, provinces and drainage now come from what happened to each place. TF24 world codes will grow different land.
- Rivers are drawn as lines with their own kinds, and soil follows them faster.
- A new Community page in the main menu, for browsing mods and worlds.
- Windows packages build again, and macOS builds and boots a world in CI.
