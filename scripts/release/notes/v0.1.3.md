**Terra Firma 0.1.3: worn, watered land, and a body on its feet**

- New worlds (TF23): land shaped by water at every scale, from great rivers down to gullies, on plates that wander and shields worn flat. Old world codes still mean what they did.
- Soil that follows the land: thin and stony on steep ground, deep in hollows and where water laid it.
- Global water networks: rivers and lakes join up across the planet.
- A place loads in about a second, and the game runs smoother: leaves hide faces you can't see, ground and physics are made ahead on worker threads.
- Loading into a world now happens over the menu's planet.
- The body stands on its feet: muscles at every joint, strides recorded from real people, a jog and run at human speeds, cleaner climbs, vaults and rolls.
- Matter: blocks melt from their open faces, powders avalanche in sixteenths, pots boil over burners.
- Windows builds.
