**Terra Firma 0.1.7: rivers cut their own valleys**

- Streams now branch across the land and carve the valleys they run in, ending on the water. TF24 world codes will grow different land.
- The far view meets the near world exactly, with fewer hitches as it loads.
- Trees and ground plants are off for now while they're rebuilt as species.
- Making a world, content and digging no longer run on Lua.
- The `terra` CLI publishes mods to Terraverse's registry and installs them from it.
