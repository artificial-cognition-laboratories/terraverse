# @terraverse/materials

The game's building materials: flint, timber, aluminium, steel, rubber, canvas and glass.
Each block says what it's made of; its weight, strength, grip and transparency come from
that matter. The first official TypeScript mod (sprint 119), ported from `tf_world`.

Its textures derive from Terra Firma's `tf_matter_substance.png` and the Good Vibes pack
(Acaitart, CC BY 4.0): see `assets/CREDITS.md` at the repository's root.
