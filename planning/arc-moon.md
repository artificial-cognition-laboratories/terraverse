# The Moon arc (sprints 92–96)

The direction (2026-11-01): a rich, plausible system from terragen, every
body with its own features, and eventually systems users build themselves
(binary planets, binary stars, many rocky worlds). So **no process is ever
chosen by name** ("this is the Moon"): the body model's causes decide which
processes run and how hard, and every body goes through the same pipeline.
A process turned to nothing simply doesn't run.

The chain: star + disk → what a body is made of → its interior (heat,
regime, field) → which surface processes run → the features.

| Sprint | What |
|---|---|
| 92 Body settings | `Surface` becomes settings from the body card (`Processes`: regime, water, life, bare ground); a stagnant lid is a one-plate history; dry bodies don't rain, erode or hold lakes |
| 93 Scale | Relief and features in metres and km (gravity and radius); atlas paging tied up (mods' places as `{body, cell}`, a walk across tiles) |
| 94 Impacts | Craters over the history: size distribution from the impact flux, bowls, central peaks, multi-ring basins, ejecta and rays, older softened under younger; regolith |
| 95 Lunar volcanism and materials | Maria flooding basins, rilles, domes; highland and mare rock; ilmenite, KREEP, ice in shadowed polar craters; shields and rifts in general |
| 96 The Moon in play | Airless sky (black, Earth in it), low gravity, no water or weather, fire failing in vacuum, travel; far view and planetarium on a cratered body |

After: a general atmosphere (greenhouse, pressure, any liquid, escape over
time: Mars's fossil rivers, Venus, Titan), wind (dunes), ice-shell worlds,
formation and the system builder, then settings past the physical.
